Mumbai Metro

Modern, air-conditioned rapid transit with high reliability, minimal traffic delays, and consistent travel times.

  • Speed: High & Reliable
  • Comfort: AC & Clean (4-5/5)
  • Cost: Moderate (₹20-₹60)
Mumbai Local Train

The lifeline of Mumbai. Unbeatable travel cost and fastest for long distances (20–40 km), but prone to extreme peak crowd.

  • Cost: Ultra Cheap (₹5-₹25)
  • Long Distance: Fastest
  • Crowd: Very High / Super Dense
Auto Rickshaw

Door-to-door suburban connectivity ideal for short routes (<8 km), but vulnerable to road congestion and high fares.

  • Convenience: Last-mile connectivity
  • Cost: High (Meter-based)
  • Traffic Impact: Severe peak delays
Core Data Science Research Questions
Which transport mode is fastest?

Analysis reveals Metro provides the lowest average travel time overall, while Local train is fastest for trips exceeding 20 km.

Which mode is cheapest?

Local trains offer the lowest cost per kilometre (averaging under ₹1.5/km), drastically cheaper than Auto rickshaws.

How does Peak Hour affect travel time?

Auto rickshaw travel times increase by 35–45% during peak hours due to road congestion, whereas Metro remains resilient.

Can Machine Learning predict the transport choice?

Using Decision Tree and Random Forest classifiers, we achieve over 95% test accuracy in mapping commuter trip parameters to suitable transport.
